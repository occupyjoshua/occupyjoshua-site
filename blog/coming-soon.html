Hello there,

My name is Joshua. Welcome to my Substack.

My background is in theology, so expect that to be the main focus of most of my musings. For now my online presence will remain somewhat anonymous, although I have yet to decide whether or not that will remain the case going forward.

I appreciate your time, as time is the one resource none of us can get back.

God bless,
